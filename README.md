## 🇬🇧 English

Screens early-stage, private-equity and startup deals from BP, deck, financials and interview notes into an auditable Screening Report. It outputs the investment thesis, red-flag list, scenarios, due-diligence gaps and a preliminary recommendation, answering whether to move to the next round or book a first meeting. Rules force identity verification, per-figure source tags, graded tables instead of assumptions when key inputs are missing, and assumption cards for magnitudes without primary sources. The report opens with an auditable summary of at most five lines. It does not replace full due diligence, legal advice or formal IC approval.

### ✨ What it does

- BP, deck, financials and interviews in → auditable screening report out
- Investment thesis plus a red-flag list of where the deal breaks
- Every precise figure carries a source tag; summary capped at five lines
- Missing key inputs become a graded table, no guessing and no refusal
- Magnitudes without primary sources go into assumption cards only
- Scenario analysis and a due-diligence gap list drive the first-meeting call
